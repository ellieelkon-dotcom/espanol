Make it yours

A small flashcard app for the words from your own lessons. How to open it, keep it on your phone, and fill it with your vocabulary.

A

Try it

Open it on your phone

ellieelkon-dotcom.github.io/espanol/en/
It asks whose words you want to practise. Tap Just try the built-in Spanish list to look around first.

Add it to your home screen

iPhone: Share ↑ → Add to Home Screen.  Android: ⋮ → Install app.
Now it opens full screen, with its own icon, and works without signal.

The first open needs internet. After that everything — your words, your streak, what you have learned — lives in that browser on that phone, and never syncs anywhere.
B

Use your own words

The app practises one list, and you choose which. Put your words in a spreadsheet and bring it in — no account, no files to edit, no code.

1. Build the sheet

SpanishTranslationExampleExample translationTopic
la mesatableLa mesa es grande.The table is big.House
nadarto swimNado cada día.I swim every day.Verbs
The first row has to name the columns. Without it the app refuses the list. Spanish and Translation are the only two it needs — it also answers to Word, Palabra, Meaning or English. The three greyed columns are optional, but an example sentence is what turns a word into a fill-in-the-blank question, so it is worth the typing.

In a hurry? The app's own setup screen has a Copy these columns to start a sheet button. Paste that into an empty spreadsheet and replace the example rows.

2. Bring it in

Paste the rows — always works

Select your rows in the spreadsheet, including the heading row, copy, and paste them into the box. Word, Google Docs, Excel and Sheets all work.

Or choose a file

A .csv, .tsv, .txt or .json saved from your spreadsheet.

Or a link, and it keeps itself up to date

Add a row after a lesson and the app has it next time — your fixes and deletions too, which pasting can never do.

For the link, an ordinary share link will not do. In the sheet: File → Share → Publish to web, pick Comma-separated values (.csv), publish, and paste that address. Paste the link from your browser's bar instead and the app answers with a wall of gibberish — that is what it looks like when a page comes back instead of a list. Publishing also means anyone holding that address can read the sheet, so keep nothing private in it.

3. Check what it read

Before anything is replaced the app shows you what it found: how many words, the first few, how many can become fill-in-the-blanks, and any row it left out and why. Nothing changes until you press Yes, use this list.

To swap lists later, or go back to the built-in Spanish one: Progress tab → Word list → Use my own word list. Each list keeps its own progress, so switching never mixes them up.

Things people ask

It says my list has no Spanish column, but it does.

Almost always the heading row was left out of the selection. The app reads the first line as the column names, so without it your first word becomes the heading.

The error prints what it read as headings — if that line shows your first word, select again from the heading row down.

Can I use it for another language?

Yes. Put any language in the Spanish column and the lists, search and quizzes all work.

The Play button is the exception: it asks your phone for a Spanish voice, so it reads your words with a Spanish accent. The grammar and listening exercises stay Spanish too, since those are built in rather than coming from your list.

Will I lose my words?

They survive closing the app, restarting, going offline. They go if you delete the app, clear your browser data, or change phone — no account is holding a copy.

A list behind a published link is its own backup. For a pasted one, keep the spreadsheet. Progress → Save a backup file stores how far you have got.

Do my words reach anyone else?

No. Your list is stored by your browser on your device and is never uploaded — there is no server behind this app to upload it to.

Two things do leave your phone: the font, fetched from Google as on most websites, and a published sheet, which the app downloads each time it opens.
